# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: plain static HTML/CSS/JS for a zero-backend personal site.

## Users

Adults who follow Queen Sam's public X presence and want to explore her adult content or request a session.

## Product Purpose

Present Queen Sam's persona, social links, services, and a bilingual way for adult visitors to prepare a booking request for follow-up through X.

## Positioning

An editorial extension of Queen Sam's dominant public persona, with findom, humiliation, and human dog-training themes.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from social media, review the profile and services, then copy a request to send in X DMs. In-person sessions are available in Bordeaux only.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static site with French default and English language toggle. Services: premade photos/videos, online sessions, in-person sessions in Bordeaux only. Prices are placeholders (`-- €`) pending confirmation. The booking form locally creates and copies a request and can open X's compose page. No backend, database, request transmission, persistence, schedule availability, or payment processing. The form asks visitors to confirm age 18+, mutual consent and limits, and that they sent the initial tribute.

## Brand Commitments

Work name: Queen Sam. Supplied description: "Your worst nightmare in a dream body." Findom specialist and humiliation expert; skilled dog trainer, with dog referring to humans in this femdom context. French and Polish. Preserve the incumbent site's visual design and broad structure. Default language is French; English is switchable.

## Product Principles

- Preserve the incumbent visual design and broad page structure.
- Show only confirmed services and supplied links.
- Keep adult-only framing, mutual consent, and agreed limits explicit.
- Do not imply that the static site books, stores, transmits, or charges for a request.
- Keep prices visibly unconfirmed until Queen Sam supplies them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Adult-only framing, semantic and labeled form controls, keyboard focus visibility, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
