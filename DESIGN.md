# Lara Cumkitten Design System

## World

The site is a private-members-club / nightlife-ledger extension of Lara's dominant public X persona. Preserve the source site's composition and art direction. The base is a dark espresso/charcoal field; bone and champagne carry the expensive feeling; rosewood is reserved for high-impact actions; gold is a hairline signal; milk blush appears only in editorial media treatment.

## Surface Context

- Surface: brand.
- Product lane: premium web presence for content creators and local businesses at Designs of Desire studio; avoid generic SaaS or corporate-template styling.
- Anti-references for new design work: cream with heavy serif display and terracotta/rust accent; near-black with one neon accent; centered gradient-blob hero with pill buttons; Inter/Poppins/Roboto for display and body; repeated three-card feature grids; arbitrary 01/02/03 numbering; generic stock photography; testimonial carousels with star ratings; four-column footer on a one-page site.
- This adaptation keeps the source design intact as explicitly requested.

## Palette

- `--black` `#0B0908` — primary obsidian night surface
- `--charcoal` `#1C1714` — secondary dark field and tribute/footer surface
- `--paper` `#F2EAD9` — bone paper surface and light type
- `--red` / `--rosewood` `#6E2E2E` — reserved high-impact CTA and statement accent
- `--gold` `#B8924A` — antique-gold hairlines, labels, link signals, and small markers
- `--foil-gold` `#D9B872` — brighter foil highlight for the hero ring and elevated markers
- `--champagne` `#D8CBB4` — large display emphasis and soft luxury detail
- `--blush` `#E9CFC7` — limited milk-blush editorial wash, never a general UI fill

## Typography

- Display: DM Serif Display, a calmer editorial serif with enough character for oversized headlines without the brittle hairlines of the previous Bodoni face.
- UI / metadata: Barlow Condensed, uppercase, tracked, compact, and assertive.
- Headings use aggressive scale and a `.82` line-height; red emphasis is applied through weight/color, not gradient text.

## Composition

- Full-width dark fields with generous vertical pacing; saturated color is concentrated into rosewood moments.
- Hero split: dominant left-aligned thesis, rotated circular mark on the right.
- Tribute links are rows, not cards: index, destination, provocation, arrow.
- Editorial tiles use supplied Lara photography.
- Footer closes with the persona's own line and only the essential outbound destinations.

## Interaction

- Primary buttons use rosewood fills; hover introduces bone/champagne contrast instead of another saturated fill.
- Buttons lift on hover and scale to `.97` on press.
- Links use short, interruptible color / transform transitions with a custom ease-out curve.
- Touch devices do not receive hover-only motion.
- Reduced-motion mode removes transform-heavy movement and preserves state changes.

## Content Rules

- Never invent services, prices, testimonials, screenshots, or private content.
- Use the supplied persona language and the stated session/content offerings.
- The adult identity should be obvious: dominance, submission, humiliation, sessions, and custom content are core editorial subjects rather than hidden subtext.
- Public-language callouts must be framed as persona language or supplied examples, never as fabricated testimonials or verified customer claims.
- Keep adult-only context visible and never suggest that this page performs payment processing itself.
